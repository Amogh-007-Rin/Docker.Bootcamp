# Module 20 — Interview Preparation
[Previous: Module 19 — Real-World Projects](../19-real-world-projects/README.md)

## What you learn
- You answer common Docker interview questions with clear, complete responses.
- You connect fundamentals to images, networking, security, and orchestration.
- You practice scenario and system-design questions with model answers.
- You use a one-page revision cheatsheet before interviews.

## Prerequisites
- You complete Modules 00–18 (or the equivalent topics from this repo).
- You finish at least Projects 01, 04, and 06–07 for realistic scenario stories.

## Estimated time
- **First pass (read + self-quiz):** 3–4 hours
- **Spaced revision:** 30 minutes daily for one week before interviews

## Files in this module

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [notes.md](notes.md) | 95+ Q&As in 10 sections + quick revision cheatsheet |
| [exercises.md](exercises.md) | Mock interview drills and self-assessment tasks |

## How to use this module
1. Read one section in `notes.md` per day; cover the answer, then answer aloud without looking.
2. For Section 10 (scenarios), time yourself: 3–5 minutes per question.
3. Complete [exercises.md](exercises.md) with a peer or record yourself explaining commands.
4. Skim the **Quick Revision Cheatsheet** at the end of `notes.md` the morning of your interview.
