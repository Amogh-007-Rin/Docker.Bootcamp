# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
You help build a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community. You treat others with respect in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Our Standards
You follow these standards:
- You use welcoming and inclusive language.
- You accept constructive feedback with humility.
- You focus on the learning goals of the repository.
- You respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

You avoid these behaviors:
- You harass, insult, or demean others.
- You share private information without consent.
- You engage in disruptive or off-topic behavior.

## Our Responsibilities
Maintainers clarify standards and enforce this code. They remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate these rules.

## Scope
This code applies within all project spaces and public community spaces. It also applies when you represent the project.

## Enforcement
You report violations to the maintainers at conduct@docker-mastery.dev. You include screenshots, links, and timestamps when possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers apply these steps consistently:
1. **Correction** — You receive a private request to change your behavior.
2. **Warning** — You receive a formal warning after repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — You receive a time-limited ban from interaction.
4. **Permanent ban** — You lose the ability to participate in the project.
